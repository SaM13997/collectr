## Design Context

### Users
Collectr’s pre-login landing page should primarily speak to general consumers who save links, posts, videos, recipes, ideas, and references across too many places, then struggle to find them again. They are not looking for a complex productivity system; they want a simple, trustworthy home for the internet things they care about. The core job is: save from anywhere now, organize only when ready, and reliably find it later across X, Reddit, Instagram, and the web.

### Brand Personality
Collectr should feel calm, expressive, and useful. Its personality is **organized, playful, and confident**: it gives users control over internet clutter without making organization feel like homework. The tone should be direct and low-pressure, favoring plain-language promises like “Save anything. Find it later.” over abstract productivity claims.

### Aesthetic Direction
For the pre-login landing page, lean into a refined editorial-brutalist direction already present in the codebase: Cabinet Grotesk headlines, sharp geometry, thick foreground borders, hard offset shadows, uppercase labels, and the existing sage / ink / coral / butter / sky palette. The page should feel more distinctive than a generic Raindrop or Pocket alternative, while still borrowing their broad consumer clarity: obvious value proposition, simple CTAs, privacy/offline trust cues, and clear save-organize-find flow. Avoid making the landing page look like a template grid, a generic SaaS hero, or an overly serious enterprise knowledge base.

### Design Principles
1. **Make internet clutter feel manageable.** Lead with the emotional relief of one searchable place for links and posts, not with implementation details.
2. **Be bold, but keep the promise simple.** Use expressive editorial typography and brutalist structure to create memorability, while keeping copy direct and consumer-friendly.
3. **Connect capture to retrieval.** Every section should reinforce the loop: save from anywhere, organize later if desired, find instantly when needed.
4. **Earn trust without fear.** Surface privacy, ownership, offline support, open-source/free positioning, and no-extension PWA convenience as quiet confidence, not defensive security theater.
5. **Refine, don’t genericize.** Keep Collectr’s sharp pre-login visual identity, but reduce repeated feature-card filler and make major surfaces feel intentionally composed rather than AI-templated.
