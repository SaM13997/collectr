## 2. Feature Descriptions

### Save from Anywhere
Paste a link from X, Reddit, Instagram, or any website. Collectr pulls the content, stores the metadata, and makes it searchable. No browser extension required — just paste or share.

### Organize Your Way
Drag items into folders. Nest folders inside folders. Add tags for cross-cutting categories. Drop notes on anything for context you'll need later. Your system, your rules.

### Full-text Search
Type a keyword, find what you saved. Full-text search across titles, descriptions, notes, and tags. No more "I know I saved that somewhere."

### Share Target
Install Collectr as a PWA and it shows up in your phone's share sheet. Share a link from any app — Twitter, Reddit, Chrome, Instagram — and it lands in Collectr instantly. One tap.

### Works Offline
Collectr is a Progressive Web App. Open it without a connection and your saved items are still there. Browse your library on a plane, in a tunnel, wherever.

### Privacy First
Your data is scoped to your account. No tracking pixels, no ad targeting, no selling your reading habits. You save it, you own it.

### Reddit Sync
Import your Reddit saved posts directly. Connect your account and pull in everything you've upvoted or bookmarked on Reddit — no manual re-saving.

### AI Enrichment
Enable optional AI auto-tagging to get suggested tags and categories when you save a link. Keeps your library organized without the manual work.

## 3. "Why Collectr?" Comparison

### vs. Browser Bookmarks
Bookmarks are buried in a menu you never open. They have no search, no tags, no notes, and they live on one device. Collectr gives you full-text search, folders, tags, and access from any device.

### vs. Pocket
Pocket is a reader. It's great for articles, but it doesn't handle tweets, Reddit posts, or Instagram links well. Collectr is source-aware — it knows what you saved and renders it appropriately. Pocket also locks features behind a paywall. Collectr doesn't.

### vs. Raindrop
Raindrop is powerful but heavy. It requires an account, a browser extension, and its free tier is limited. Collectr is a PWA — install it, share to it, done. No extension needed. Fully open source.

### vs. Notion Web Clipper
Notion is a workspace, not a bookmark manager. Clipping a link into Notion means creating a database entry, setting properties, and hoping the template works. Collectr is purpose-built: paste a link, it's saved. Organize later if you want.

**Collectr's edge:**
- Multi-source support (X, Reddit, Instagram, any URL)
- PWA share target — works from any mobile app
- No browser extension required
- Open source and free
- Fast, minimal, no bloat
